## Where you can withdraw cash

You can withdraw cash at any Visa-enabled ATM worldwide using your physical card,
and at supported ATMs using Apple Pay or Google Pay. NovaBank has no branches and
does not offer over-the-counter withdrawals.

## Free allowance and withdrawal fees

Withdrawals are free up to the free ATM allowance of GBP 200 in any rolling 30-day
period, across all currencies and countries combined. Above the allowance, NovaBank
charges the ATM withdrawal fee of 2% of the withdrawn amount, minimum GBP 1. The
allowance and the fee are the same at home and abroad. ATM operators may add their
own surcharge, which is shown on the machine before you confirm and is not charged
by NovaBank.

## Withdrawal limits

The daily ATM limit is GBP 500 or the local-currency equivalent, and the per-30-day
ATM limit is GBP 3,000. Individual ATMs may impose lower per-transaction limits.

## Withdrawal declined at an ATM

Typical causes: the daily ATM limit was reached; the machine's own limit is lower
than the amount requested; the PIN is blocked (see `card-activation-and-pin`); the
card is frozen; the ATM cannot reach the Visa network; or the ATM is out of cash.
A declined withdrawal does not debit your balance, but some ATMs briefly place a
pending authorisation that clears within 24 hours.

## Withdrawal still pending

An ATM withdrawal shows as pending until the ATM operator settles it, normally
within 3 business days and always within 7 days. The money is already reserved from
your available balance while pending. If a pending withdrawal has not settled after
7 days it expires automatically and the reserved money returns to your balance.

## Wrong amount of cash received

If an ATM dispensed less than it debited, report it within 30 days: open the
transaction > Something's wrong > Wrong amount received, and give the amount you
actually received. NovaBank raises an ATM dispute with the operator, who audits the
machine's cash balance. Operator audits take 10 to 20 business days, longer than a
card dispute, because they depend on the physical cash reconciliation. If the audit
confirms a shortfall, the difference is refunded in full.

## Foreign currency withdrawals

Withdrawing in a currency you do not hold converts from your GBP balance at the
rate in `exchange-rates-and-fx`. Always choose to be charged in the local currency:
if you accept the ATM's own conversion (dynamic currency conversion), the ATM
operator's rate applies instead of NovaBank's, and it is almost always worse.
