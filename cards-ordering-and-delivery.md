## Card types NovaBank issues

NovaBank issues one physical Visa debit card per customer, plus virtual cards.
NovaBank does not issue Mastercard, credit cards or cheque books. Physical card
production begins once identity verification is complete.

## Ordering a physical card

Order from Cards > Order physical card. The first physical card is free. Each
additional or replacement card costs the card replacement fee defined in
`fees-and-charges`. A spare card (a second active card on the same account) can be
ordered for the same fee and has its own card number and PIN.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery is 3 to 5 business days within the United Kingdom and 7 to 10
business days to other supported countries. Express delivery (1 to 2 business
days, UK only) is available for the express delivery fee in `fees-and-charges`.
Delivery times start from the dispatch confirmation, not from the order time.

## Card has not arrived

If a standard UK card has not arrived after 10 business days, or an international
card after 15 business days, report it in the app: Cards > Track card > Report not
received. NovaBank cancels the card in transit and issues a free replacement. Do
not report a card missing before those windows have passed — cards already in the
postal network cannot be traced.

## Tracking a card

Cards > Track card shows the dispatch date and current status: `preparing`,
`dispatched`, `delivered_expected`. NovaBank does not provide courier tracking
numbers for standard delivery. Express deliveries include a courier reference.

## Card expiry and renewal

Cards are valid for 3 years. A renewal card is dispatched automatically 30 days
before expiry to your registered address, at no cost. Check your address is
current at least 45 days before expiry. The old card stops working at midnight on
the last day of the expiry month; the renewal card must be activated separately.
