## Immediate action: freeze the card

If your card is lost, stolen or you believe someone else has the details, freeze it
immediately in Cards > Freeze card. Freezing is instant and reversible, blocks all
new authorisations, and does not affect existing direct debits. If you cannot access
the app, call the 24/7 lost and stolen line on 0800 000 0000.

## Reporting it and ordering a replacement

After freezing, choose Report lost or stolen. This permanently cancels the card
number so it cannot be unfrozen, and starts a replacement. One replacement per
12-month period is free; further replacements cost the card replacement fee in
`fees-and-charges`. Delivery follows the standard times in
`cards-ordering-and-delivery`.

## Card swallowed by an ATM

If an ATM retains your card, freeze it in the app and contact the ATM operator
(the machine displays their number) — NovaBank cannot recover a card from another
bank's ATM. Cards retained by an ATM are destroyed by the operator, so order a
replacement. If the ATM was NovaBank-branded, report it in the app and the
replacement fee is waived.

## Lost or stolen phone

A lost phone does not expose your money on its own: the app requires your passcode
or biometrics. Sign in on a new device and choose "This is my new device", then
de-register the old one in Profile > Devices. If the phone had a wallet card
(Apple Pay or Google Pay) provisioned, remove it from Cards > Digital wallets.
Contact your mobile operator to block the SIM, because SMS one-time codes are sent
to it.

## Suspected compromised card details

If you have not lost the card but suspect the number was copied (for example after
a data breach or a skimmed ATM), freeze the card and choose Report compromised.
NovaBank issues a new card number free of charge and reviews the last 90 days of
transactions. Any transactions you did not authorise should be disputed following
`duplicate-and-unrecognised-charges`.

## Liability

You are not liable for unauthorised transactions made after you report a card lost,
stolen or compromised. For transactions before you reported it, NovaBank refunds
unauthorised payments unless there is evidence of gross negligence, such as sharing
your PIN, passcode or a one-time code with another person. NovaBank staff will
never ask for your PIN, passcode or one-time code.
