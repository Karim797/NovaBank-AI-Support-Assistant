## What NovaBank will never ask for

NovaBank will never ask for your PIN, app passcode, one-time code, full card number
or the CVV on the back of your card. NovaBank will never ask you to move money to a
"safe account" — no such thing exists, and every request of that kind is a scam.
Staff will never ask you to install remote-access software.

## If you think you have been scammed

Freeze the card immediately and report it in the app (Help > Report fraud) or call
the 24/7 line on 0800 000 0000. Speed matters: a payment can sometimes be recalled
in the first hours. Do not delete messages or call logs — they are evidence.

## Unauthorised transactions

An unauthorised transaction is one you did not make or approve. Report it as soon
as you notice, and always within 13 months. NovaBank refunds unauthorised
transactions within 1 business day of the report unless it has reasonable grounds
to suspect fraud by you or gross negligence, in which case it investigates first
and responds within 15 business days.

## Authorised push payment (APP) scams

If you were tricked into sending a payment yourself, report it the same way.
NovaBank is a signatory to the UK reimbursement rules for APP scams: eligible
claims are assessed within 5 business days and reimbursed unless you ignored a
specific warning shown during the payment or acted with gross negligence.

## Keeping the account secure

Enable biometrics, keep the app updated, never share one-time codes, review the
device list in Profile > Devices, and use disposable virtual cards (see
`virtual-and-disposable-cards`) for one-off online purchases. NovaBank sends
security alerts only through the app and never includes a link to log in.

## Escalating to a human

Fraud reports are always reviewed by a human specialist, not automatically. Any
customer can ask to speak to a person at any point in Help > Contact us; the fraud
line is answered 24/7 and other queries within 1 hour during 08:00 to 20:00 UK
time.
