## Why NovaBank verifies your identity

UK anti-money-laundering regulation requires NovaBank to confirm who its customers
are before providing an account, and to keep that information current. Verification
also protects you: it is what stops someone else opening an account in your name.
NovaBank cannot waive verification for any customer, for any reason.

## What you need to provide

A valid photo ID (passport, national ID card, or UK photocard driving licence) and
a live selfie taken in the app. The document must be unexpired, fully visible in
frame, and match the name on your application. Screenshots, photocopies and photos
of a screen are rejected automatically.

## How long it takes

Automated checks complete in under 5 minutes in most cases. If a check needs manual
review, the outcome takes up to 2 business days and you are notified in the app. You
cannot use the account, spend, or withdraw while verification is outstanding.

## When verification keeps failing

You get 3 automated attempts. Common causes of failure are glare on the document,
a cropped edge, poor lighting on the selfie, an expired document, or a name
mismatch after a legal name change. After 3 failures the case goes to manual review
automatically — do not create a second account, which will be closed and can delay
the review. Manual review may request an additional proof of address dated within
the last 3 months (bank statement, utility bill, council tax letter).

## Source of funds

NovaBank asks for source of funds evidence when incoming money is large or does not
fit your stated account use. The standard triggers are: a single credit above GBP
10,000; cumulative credits above GBP 25,000 in a rolling 30 days; or any credit from
a jurisdiction on the enhanced due-diligence list. Acceptable evidence includes
payslips, a sale contract, an inheritance letter from a solicitor, a tax return, or
signed company accounts. Screenshots of another bank's app are not accepted.

## While a check is open

Funds subject to a source of funds check are held and cannot be spent or withdrawn.
Reviews complete within 5 business days of receiving complete evidence. NovaBank
cannot always explain the detail of a check: where a legal restriction applies,
support can confirm a review is open but not its reasoning.
