## Charged twice for the same purchase

A transaction that appears twice is usually one settled payment plus one pending
authorisation that has not yet expired. Check the transaction states first: if one
entry is pending, it will normally drop off within the authorisation window in
`pending-and-failed-transactions` and only one charge will settle.

If both entries have settled, report it in the app: open either transaction >
Something's wrong > Charged twice. NovaBank contacts the merchant, and the
investigation SLA is 5 business days. Where the duplicate is confirmed by the
merchant, a provisional refund is credited within 2 business days of confirmation.

## Time limit for reporting

Report a duplicate or incorrect charge within 60 days of the transaction date.
After 60 days NovaBank can still raise a claim but the Visa chargeback rights in
`refunds-and-chargebacks` may already have expired, which materially reduces the
chance of recovery.

## Extra charge on your statement

An unexpected extra charge is most often one of: a foreign exchange charge (see
`exchange-rates-and-fx`), an ATM withdrawal fee above the free allowance (see
`cash-withdrawals-and-atms`), a card replacement or express delivery fee (see
`fees-and-charges`), or a merchant adding a service charge, tip or fuel-pump
pre-authorisation. Open the transaction to see the fee breakdown, which lists the
NovaBank fee separately from the merchant amount.

## Transactions you do not recognise at all

Before disputing, check the merchant's registered trading name and the date. If it
is still unrecognised, freeze the card and choose Report unrecognised transaction.
NovaBank treats this as potential fraud: the card is reissued free of charge and
the transaction is investigated under `fraud-and-security`. Unauthorised
transactions are refunded within 1 business day of the report unless NovaBank has
reasonable grounds to suspect the claim is false.

## What NovaBank needs from you

For a merchant dispute: the transaction, what you expected, and any evidence
(order confirmation, cancellation email, delivery tracking). Claims raised without
evidence take longer and are more often rejected by the merchant's bank. Do not
also raise a claim directly with the merchant at the same time — a duplicate claim
can cause both refunds to be reversed.
