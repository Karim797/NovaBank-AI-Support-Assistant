## Merchant refunds

When a merchant issues a refund, it reaches your NovaBank balance in 5 to 10
business days. NovaBank credits the money on the day it is received from the card
scheme and cannot speed it up: the delay is at the merchant and their acquiring
bank. Refunds return to the card used for the purchase, even if that card has since
been replaced or cancelled — the money still reaches your account.

## Refund not showing up

If a merchant confirmed a refund more than 10 business days ago and it has not
arrived, ask the merchant for the Acquirer Reference Number (ARN). With the ARN,
NovaBank can trace the payment: open the original transaction > Something's wrong >
Refund not received, and enter the ARN. Tracing takes up to 5 business days.
Without an ARN a refund cannot be traced.

## Chargeback: what it is

A chargeback is a Visa scheme process that forcibly reclaims money from the
merchant's bank. It is used when the merchant will not refund you and one of the
scheme reasons applies: goods or services never received, not as described,
duplicate processing, cancelled recurring payment, or an unauthorised transaction.

## Chargeback time limits

Visa allows a chargeback up to 120 days from the transaction date, or from the date
goods or services were expected, whichever is later, with an absolute cap of 540
days from the transaction. NovaBank must receive your claim at least 15 days before
the scheme deadline to be able to raise it.

## Chargeback process and timeline

1. NovaBank raises the chargeback and credits a provisional refund within 2 business days.
2. The merchant's bank has up to 30 days to accept or defend the claim.
3. If defended, NovaBank reviews the evidence and may escalate to pre-arbitration.
4. Final outcomes are usually reached within 45 to 90 days from the claim date.

If the merchant successfully defends the claim, the provisional refund is reversed
and you will be notified at least 5 business days before the money is taken back.

## Chargeback is not always the right route

If the goods arrived but you simply changed your mind, the merchant's returns
policy applies and a chargeback will be rejected. For a purchase over GBP 100 paid
partly or fully by credit card, Section 75 protection may apply — NovaBank issues
debit cards only, so Section 75 does not apply to NovaBank payments.
