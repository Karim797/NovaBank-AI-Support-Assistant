## How do I contact a human?

Help > Contact us in the app. The fraud and lost-card line (0800 000 0000) is
answered 24 hours a day. All other queries are answered in-app within 1 hour
between 08:00 and 20:00 UK time, and by the next morning outside those hours.

## Is my money protected?

Eligible deposits are protected up to GBP 85,000 per person by the UK Financial
Services Compensation Scheme. Money held in EUR or USD balances is protected at the
GBP equivalent on the date of any claim.

## Does NovaBank offer overdrafts, loans or credit cards?

No. NovaBank offers a current account with debit cards only. There is no overdraft
facility, so a payment that would take the balance below zero is declined rather
than accepted at a fee.

## Does NovaBank support business accounts?

Not currently. NovaBank accounts are for personal use. Using a personal account for
business trading breaches the account terms and may lead to closure.

## Complaints

Raise a complaint in Help > Complaints. NovaBank acknowledges within 3 business days
and responds within 8 weeks. If you are unhappy with the outcome, or after 8 weeks,
you can refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service free of charge.

## Where policies are published

The full terms and conditions, fee schedule and privacy notice are in the app under
Profile > Legal documents. Any material change is notified at least 60 days in
advance.
