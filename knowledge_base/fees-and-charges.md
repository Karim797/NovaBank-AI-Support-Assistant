## Fee schedule (authoritative)

This section is the single source of truth for NovaBank fees. Any other document
refers to these fees by name.

| Fee name | Amount |
|---|---|
| Account maintenance | Free |
| First physical card | Free |
| Card replacement fee | GBP 5 (first replacement in any 12 months is free) |
| Express delivery fee (UK) | GBP 15 |
| ATM withdrawal fee | 2% of amount above the free ATM allowance, minimum GBP 1 |
| Free ATM allowance | GBP 200 per rolling 30 days |
| Card payments (domestic and foreign) | Free — no NovaBank transaction fee |
| FX conversion fee | 0.5% above the free FX allowance |
| Free FX allowance | GBP 1,000 per calendar month |
| Weekend FX markup | 0.5% on conversions Saturday and Sunday |
| Faster Payments / SEPA transfer | Free |
| International (SWIFT) transfer fee | GBP 6 per payment |
| Card top-up fee | 1% above the free card top-up allowance |
| Free card top-up allowance | GBP 2,000 per rolling 30 days |
| Bank transfer top-up | Free |
| Receiving money | Free |
| Chargeback or dispute | Free |
| Account closure | Free |

## Fees NovaBank does not charge

NovaBank does not charge for: monthly account maintenance, minimum balance, card
payments in any currency, declined transactions, holding EUR or USD balances,
paper statements (there are none — statements are digital), or customer support.

## Why a fee appeared on your statement

Every NovaBank fee is shown as its own line item with the fee name from the table
above, never bundled into the merchant amount. If a charge is not one of the named
fees above, it was not charged by NovaBank — see
`duplicate-and-unrecognised-charges`.

## Fee refunds

A fee charged in error is refunded in full within 2 business days of the error
being confirmed. Fees correctly applied under this schedule are not refundable,
except that the card replacement fee is waived if the card failed through a
manufacturing fault or was retained by a NovaBank-branded ATM.
