## Who can open a NovaBank account

NovaBank is an app-only bank. To open a Standard account you must be 18 or older
and resident in a supported country. Applicants aged 16 or 17 can open a Youth
account, which has no overdraft, no international transfers and a GBP 500 monthly
spending cap. Youth accounts convert to Standard automatically on the customer's
18th birthday after identity re-verification.

## Supported countries

NovaBank currently supports residents of the United Kingdom, Ireland, France,
Germany, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands and Poland. You must have a residential
address in a supported country and a valid national ID or passport. Customers who
move to an unsupported country must close the account within 60 days of the move.

## Account currencies

Every account is opened with a GBP balance. EUR and USD balances can be added from
the app at no cost, up to three currency balances per customer. Balances are held
separately; money is only converted between them when you request an exchange.
See `exchange-rates-and-fx` for conversion pricing.

## Editing personal details

Name, address, phone number and email can be updated in Profile > Personal
details. Address and phone changes trigger a 24-hour cooling-off period during
which new payees cannot be added. A legal name change requires a supporting
document (marriage certificate, deed poll) and is reviewed within 2 business days.

## Closing an account

You can close an account from Profile > Account > Close account. The balance must
be zero: transfer remaining funds out first, or request a closing payment to a
verified bank account in your own name. Closure completes within 5 business days.
Statements remain downloadable for 12 months after closure, and NovaBank retains
transaction records for 6 years as required by UK regulation.
